A search for “Invokyr achievements” should land on a straight answer. The answer, checked 21 Oct 2026, is that Steam is not tracking any.

## What was checked

The public stats URL for this AppID returned the Steam community error “No stats are available at this time.” That is the page that normally lists global achievement percentages.

The store API category list for the same app, the same day, was:

- Single-player, multi-player, co-op, online co-op
- Adjustable text size, custom volume, playable without timed input, stereo, subtitle options
- Family sharing

Steam Achievements, Steam Cloud, and Steam Workshop were not in that list. Their absence is the evidence. It is not a hidden menu this guide failed to open.

<Callout kind="warn" title="Do not trust a invented checklist">
Some fan pages publish achievement titles, rarity percentages, and biome mastery trophies. Those names do not appear in Ludogram’s patch notes or on the Steam stats page. Treating them as unlock steps will waste a run. If the studio ships a real set, the stats URL above will start returning rows, and this page should be rewritten from that schema.
</Callout>

## Milestones that are actually in the game

These are not achievements. They are the progression Ludogram has described.

| Gate | Source | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear a board | Launch post and the shop guide | The shop opens, then restocks after later clears |
| Beat the Game Master board | Patch V0.44.12, 17 Oct 2026 | Custom Mode becomes available |
| Pull the lever in the collection room | Same patch | You may mix unlocked boards, House Rules, and monster figurines |
| Win or lose in Custom Mode | Same patch | You return to the last round. No payout, no death penalty |

Monster figurines in that mode are a collection you unlock by playing. The patch does not say a figurine pops a Steam badge. Player stats on portraits, added in V0.44.11, are a HUD change, not an achievement.

## If you want the completionist path anyway

Play the four launch themes, then the Master Board, then open Custom Mode and see which figurines and House Rules the collection room actually offers you. That is the checklist the client can show. Write down what you unlock. This site will not guess the missing rule numbers players were already asking about in October 2026.
