If you came here for a tier list of characters, the store page will disappoint you on purpose. Invokyr’s Early Access pitch is the board, not a fighter select.

![Capsule art of two players at the table](/images/official/steam/capsule-616x353.jpg)

## What the studio shipped instead of classes

The 8 Oct 2026 launch post spends its feature list on boards, monsters, events, dice, cursed idols, House Rules, and the shop. It never names a playable character. The store categories are single-player, multi-player, co-op, and online co-op, plus text size, volume, subtitles, stereo, and family sharing. No “classes” tag exists to hide a roster behind.

The capsule art shows people in ordinary clothes at the table. The community shop list matches that: you buy a sweater color, you do not unlock a third protagonist.

## What can still make two players different

| Difference | Where it comes from | Shared? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum health, the stamina purchase, run speed | Shop upgrades, buyer only | No |
| Extra inventory from the Fanny Pack | Shop, two stacks | No |
| A full heal from the Milk Bottle | Shop, one use | No |
| Gun, flamethrower, mask, goggles, compass | Gadgets. The guide describes them as things a player uses | The price is one player’s purchase. The guide does not say the whole table receives a gun |
| Clock, mirror, disco ball, cube dispenser | Furniture in the game room | Yes |
| Wallpaper and sweater | Cosmetics. Not kept across a save reset | The wallpaper is the house. The sweater is yours |

V0.44.11 started drawing player stats on the portraits in the game room. That is the client’s own summary of whoever has bought what. Prefer it to a screenshot of this table after the next balance patch.

<Callout kind="note" title="Table jobs are not roles">
Groups still split the work: one person watches a mannequin, one carries the idol, one reads the rhyme, one holds the Thread die for the walk home. Those are decisions. Nothing in the news or the guide turns them into selectable classes with passives.
</Callout>

## The Game Master

The store story is a 1970s board game in an abandoned house. A beloved toy is now the Gamemaster, and escape means playing. The final idol is that figure’s soul. Sealing it is the win condition the community guide and the launch post agree on.

The Game Master Mask, in the shop list, lets you wear that face and pass enemies for a limited charge that refills between boards. It does not make you the host of the board. Custom Mode, after you have beaten the Game Master board, lets the table pick monster figurines. You are still the players. You are choosing which hunter the collection remembers.

More lore about the house and the Game Master is on the Early Access roadmap, not in a character bio. The Henry notes on [Secrets](/secrets) are the side story the community guide has actually transcribed.
