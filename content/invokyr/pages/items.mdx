Money on this page is the guide’s dollar sign, the same unit as idol values. It is not a Steam wallet price. A 30% tag is a chance, not the price you should expect.

## Upgrades

These apply only to the player who buys them. They reset when a new game starts. The numbers are “after the first board,” and they climb from there.

| Upgrade | Effect in the guide | Stacks | Price (sale) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Health | +25 maximum health | 8 | 200 (140) |
| Stamina | “Increases maximum health by one bar” | 7 | 200 (140) |
| Speed | Faster running | 5 | 200 (140) |
| Fanny Pack | One more inventory slot | 2 | 400 (280) |
| Milk Bottle | One use. Full heal for the buyer | Not a stack | 100 |

<Callout kind="warn" title="Stamina’s description disagrees with its name">
The guide says the stamina upgrade increases maximum health by one bar. That may mean a stamina bar, and it may be a slip. Read the tooltip in the shop before you buy a stack of seven.
</Callout>

## Furniture

Furniture is placed in the game room. Anyone can use it.

| Item | What the guide says | Price (sale) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Old Clock | Looks broken until you buy it. A full team wipe sends you to the shop instead of ending the run. One use. Buy it again after it breaks | 2000 (1400) |
| Mirror | Unlocks the game-room mirror. Works like the mirrors in the house. Once per board | 750 (525) |
| Disco Ball | Lights the game room. No mechanical effect is listed | 1500 (1050) |
| Cube Dice Dispenser | Hands out Cube dice | 500 (350) |

## Gadgets

Some are tools. Some are noise. Limited ammo and limited charge reload between boards when the guide says so.

| Gadget | Effect in the guide | Price (sale) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compass | Points at the game room | 1250 (875) |
| Hunter Gun | A gun. Limited ammo | 2500 (1750) |
| Flamethrower | Sets fire to what is in front of you. Limited ammo | 2500 (1750) |
| Game Master Mask | Wear the Gamemaster’s face and pass enemies unnoticed. Limited charge | 1750 (1225) |
| X-Ray Goggles | See players and the rooms through walls. Limited charge | 1250 (875) |
| Player Skull | Talk to dead teammates from anywhere | 500 (350) |
| Camera | Take pictures | 375 (262) |
| Monster Sound Box | Plays monster sounds | 250 (175) |
| Megaphone | Makes the planning voice louder | 250 (175) |

V0.44.11 fixed a case where a save did not load upgrades. If a purchase vanishes after a reload, that bug is the one the studio said it closed. Check the portrait stats anyway. The same patch started showing player stats on portraits.

## Cosmetics

Wallpaper is on the back wall of the shop, 200, and changes the manor. Sweaters hang on a rack: standard colors 25, gold sweater 1500. The guide says cosmetics are not permanent. A new save means you buy them again.

## What the studio has promised later

The launch roadmap says the shop will get “major improvements,” including new gadgets, furniture, and features, after the team finishes the first round of bug fixes. Nothing in V0.44.7 through V0.44.13 is a new shop catalogue. Prices above can still be wrong in your client if a silent balance pass landed. The DiceBurner payouts are on [Dice](/dice).
