The store line is “work together… or leave someone behind.” Both are possible. The systems below are how a table chooses.

## Seats and lobbies

One to six players. The 10 Aug 2026 demo update added public lobbies and Steam invites. Early Access kept a lobby browser: V0.44.9 fixed it, V0.44.11 let the host kick, and V0.44.13 fixed a case where some public lobbies never appeared. The same patch warns on the join screen if your build is old. Match versions before you debug a softlock.

V0.44.11’s networking note is specific. Steam networking stays the default. The Epic Online Services option is for people who already cannot connect. It is not a graphics setting.

V0.44.10 made disconnects less cruel in one way and more honest in another: fewer softlocks, endless spectate states, and duplicate turns, and a disconnected player now drops what they were carrying. Say so before you pull a cable. Someone has to walk the idol back.

## A useful split, with no classes required

- **One pair of eyes on the mannequin.** The guide’s group tactic is one player staring so the others can pass. Solo, you back up until it leaves your view and goes still.
- **One carrier, one navigator.** The idol holder is the Money Man’s target. The navigator calls doors and does not also need to be the person with inverted controls.
- **One die for the walk home.** Thread, Rally, or a Teleport can save the soul carry. Do not spend the last Resurrect die on a joke.
- **Call the rhyme out loud.** Half the table is in another room when the token stops. The rhyme is the only warning they get.
- **Do not all sprint.** Plants, the huntsman, and the sandworm are documented as waking up to movement or noise. Six sets of footsteps are louder than one.

<Callout kind="note" title="Voice is a mechanic">
Player Mimics repeat lines from proximity chat. A shared-healing curse means one person’s mistake is the table’s health bar. The megaphone in the shop is the guide’s tool for making a plan audible. The spoken “Invokyr” at the end is still only documented in the community guide.
</Callout>

## When someone is dead

| Way back | Limit in the guide |
| --- | --- |
| Mirror in the house | Save your image first. One revive, then the mirror breaks |
| Game-room mirror | Shop furniture, 750, once per board |
| Resurrect die | One random dead player, even from an earlier layout. Bad roll spawns a mimic. Dying while you carry one consumes it and revives you |
| Heal die | Revives anyone inside the circle at 10 HP, or heals the living for 50 |
| Portraits | The dead can speak through the game-room portraits to anyone standing close |
| Whisper | The dead fill a meter and whisper to one player they are watching |
| Player Skull | Shop gadget, 500. Talk to the dead from anywhere |
| Old Clock | Not a revive. If the whole team dies, you return to the shop instead of losing the run. One use |
| Final event | Reaching the center revives the dead for the soul carry |

V0.44.8 is the save patch: hosting always opens save selection, including a “New Save” option when you have none, the pause menu shows how long ago the run was saved, and the Game Saved notice stays up longer. This page does not repeat an unverified claim that a death mid-board deletes the file. The clock is the failure tool the guide actually describes.

## Solo

Single-player is a store category, not a rumor. You will not have a lookout, a second pair of hands for the lava boxes, or a teammate whose eyes the Other Player Eyes idol can borrow. That idol becomes a Normal idol if nobody else is alive. Take the early boards before you stack House Rules. Custom Mode will not pay you for practicing.
