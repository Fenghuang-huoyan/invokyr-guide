The rhymes are the in-game announcement, copied here from Eisenfang’s transcription so you can match a line you just heard. Behavior under each line is that guide’s description, not a second source.

## Monster spawns

These stay for the rest of the game. How to survive the creature is on [Monsters](/monsters). Knights are described there and do not have a rhyme in this list.

| Event | Rhyme in the guide |
| --- | --- |
| Alligator | Keep your feet up above the mud / or it will bite you in cold blood |
| Carnivorous Plant | Hidden in leaves, it lays in slumber / slow down your pace or make a blunder |
| Dress Mannequins | Red light, green light, they want to play / they know when you’re looking away |
| Huntsman | He sees no light, but hears it all / one breath, one step, and down you fall |
| Ice Miner | Once he tangles you in his rope / breaking free is your only hope |
| Mimic Door | A fake door waits with hungry breath / listen up first or step to death |
| Money Man | Guarding his bank, looking for gold / don’t let him notice what you hold |
| Mummy | Don’t trip over his bandages / or face his wrath of past ages |
| Player Mimics | One of you may not be true / stay together, or they’ll come for you |
| Penguins | A cranky bunch to babysit / no way out once the fuse is lit |
| Sandworm | Don’t let your footsteps make a sound / something is lurking underground |
| Shadow Man | Don’t let the TVs play the tape / or let the Shadow Man escape |
| Snowmen | Copies gather, harmless and cold / run from the one who breaks the mold |
| Spiders | On the ceiling, they walk around / look up above, or end up bound |
| Yeti | A trail of footsteps in the snow / a roar before a deadly blow |

<Callout kind="warn" title="Ice Miner is a rhyme without a paragraph">
The guide tells you the miner spawns and that the rope has to be broken. It does not say how long the snare lasts, what breaks it, or what the miner looks like. If your group has a reliable method, it is newer than 19 Oct 2026.
</Callout>

## Set pieces

| Event | What you have to do |
| --- | --- |
| Ballerina | Everyone is sent to a room with an idol. A ballerina spins in the room you must cross. Move while she is stopped and spikes come up. |
| Chessboard | An idol on the far side of a giant board. Pieces move and try to crush anyone on a square. |
| Dice Spawn | The board throws several dice. They count as already rolled, good or bad. |
| Fire Pillar | A pillar in the middle of the room sprays long jets and reverses direction. It sinks when the event ends. Stay out of the jets. |
| Flash Freeze | The game-room floor stays icy for the rest of the game. |
| Mannequin Attic | A door opens to a cramped attic full of dress mannequins. Idols are at the back. Someone has to keep watch. |
| Rolling Boulder | The center door opens on one idol. Picking it up starts a boulder that chases that player to the game room. If they die, the boulder does not return for the next person who grabs the idol. V0.44.9 made this “slightly easier,” without saying how. |
| Shell Game | Three doors, three cards shuffling above them. Enter the door under the idol card. The other two are mimics. The 8 Oct patch calls this the Bonneteau event and says an exploit was closed. |
| The Floor is Lava | Rising lava, large boxes, a balcony. The guide says you need 10 boxes stacked 1-2-3-4. Afterward, lava still surges over the floor for a few seconds at a time. You get a fire cue. Stand on a box or a table. |
| Trapdoor Game | A 5×5 floor. A wheel picks the safe symbol. Every other tile opens. Three rounds, and more symbols join each round. |
| Game Completion | Always at the center. The rhyme is “At last you found my long lost hearse / retrieve my soul and end the curse.” Dead players revive. Someone walks the soul idol back and seals it. |

The Inverted Controls idol no longer spawns during the boulder, as of V0.44.7. That is the only event-specific idol ban this site has from a patch note.
